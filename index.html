Before you donate

Donate safely.

Community fridges work best when donations are clean, safe, clearly labeled, and useful to the next person.

01 Good to donate

Helpful items

These are usually useful choices when they are fresh, unopened, and stored correctly.

  • ✓
    Sealed packaged food

    Unopened food with clear labels and packaging.

  • ✓
    Fresh produce

    Fruits and vegetables that are clean and in good condition.

  • ✓
    Unexpired canned food

    Cans that are unopened and not heavily damaged.

  • ✓
    Unopened drinks

    Bottled water, juice, and other sealed drinks.

  • ✓
    Clearly labeled refrigerated food

    Items that are properly sealed and safe to place inside the fridge.

02 Do not donate

Items to avoid

Do not leave anything that could be unsafe, unclear, damaged, or difficult for volunteers to manage.

  • ×
    Open containers

    Food or drinks that have already been opened.

  • ×
    Expired or spoiled food

    Anything expired, moldy, damaged, or unsafe to eat.

  • ×
    Leaking packages

    Containers that are broken, swollen, leaking, or badly dented.

  • ×
    Unlabeled food

    Items without ingredients, dates, or clear packaging.

  • ×
    Items against posted rules

    Always follow the instructions displayed at each fridge.

!

Check before donating. Rules may be different at each community fridge. Read the signs posted at the location and check recent community updates before leaving food.
